READING NOTESThree claims this table does not support

  • S0
    The framework port is not a contribution. M1G screens candidate orders in advance, keeping only those the current stock can satisfy in full; this framework instead refuses the reward inside the objective and works from residual rather than original demand — which is what allows a partly served order to return in a later period. The two candidate pools are therefore not comparable, and attribution starts at S1.
  • PRICES
    Measured prices do not raise throughput. Their +2.16% mostly returns what the preceding step gave up; the net gain across both is +0.64%. What they do deliver is efficiency: pile-on +34.00% and 22.81% fewer station arrivals.
  • S2–S3
    The two-layer objective must not be shown alone. On its own it reads as a 1.52% throughput loss. It is the carrier that makes a price measurable, and it is inseparable from the step that follows — the pair is what produces the result.
